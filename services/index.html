Team Collaboration Model

How We Work Together

Clear roles, mutual creative respect, and guaranteed festival credits. Transparent collaboration for short film projects.

What You Get as a Collaborator

1. Guaranteed Screen Credit

Prominent screen credit in both opening and ending titles (e.g. "Jane Doe — Screenplay") across all festival screenings and online distribution.

2. Official Deal Memo

A formal bilateral collaboration agreement signed before production commences, protecting your moral authorship rights.

3. International Distribution

Completed films are targeted for European and international film festival submissions, youth showcases, and high-definition public releases.

4. Production Master Files

High-resolution ProRes video masters, pristine stills, and audio stems for your personal showreel and acting/directing portfolio.

Roles Currently Needed

📜

Screenwriters & Co-Writers

Writers with short narrative scripts (5–15 pages) featuring strong visual dialogue, psychological tension, or atmospheric winter drama.

🎭

Actors & Performers

Performers comfortable with subtle, nuanced on-camera acting and emotional restraint. Open to actors from Lithuania and internationally.

🎬

Co-Directors & Story Editors

Creative directors wanting a dedicated cinematographer and editor to bring their visual vision to life on location or remotely.

🎵

Sound Designers & Composers

Musicians and audio engineers specializing in organic ambient textures, silence dynamics, and cinematic atmospheric scores.

The 4-Step Production Process

1

Pitch or Role Application

You fill out the submission form with your screenplay, pitch, or reel. I review every entry personally within 48 hours.

2

Creative Consultation & Agreement

We connect via Telegram or video call to align on visual tone, timeline, and sign our mutual deal memo guarantee.

3

Filming & Post-Production

Production takes place on location or remotely. I handle camera direction, cinematography, rough cut, color grading, and audio mastering.

4

Festival Submissions & Distribution

The film is submitted to international festivals and celebrated online with full credits and portfolio assets for everyone.

Technical Capabilities

Informational list of technical production services I provide for collaborating directors and creators:

  • 🎥 Cinematography: Lighting setups, camera operating, mobile rigs, framing, drone cinematography.
  • 🎬 Editing & Pacing: Non-linear editing, narrative assembly, scene rhythm, trailer cuts.
  • 🎨 Color Grading: DaVinci Resolve color pipelines, filmic emulation, and mood-matching.
