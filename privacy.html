2. What Information We Collect

When you choose to submit a film proposal, screenplay, or application via our recruitment form, we collect:

  • Identification details: Your full name.
  • Geographic location: Country and city/region.
  • Filmmaking preferences: Your role (Screenwriter, Actor, Director, Sound Designer, Crew) and proposal type.
  • Contact details: Your chosen communication handle (Email, Telegram, Instagram, or Phone/WhatsApp).
  • Message content: Pitch synopses, creative vision, links to reels or portfolios, and any attached PDF files (treatments, screenplays).

3. Purpose and Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your personal data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Evaluating creative proposals for short film production collaboration.
  • Direct bilateral communication regarding auditions, script review, or production scheduling.
  • Drafting official bilateral film collaboration agreements and festival screen credit documentation.

Legal Basis: Processing is based on your explicit, affirmative consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), granted by selecting the consent checkbox prior to submitting the form.

4. Data Processors & Infrastructure

To securely deliver and manage communications, your data is processed by trusted cloud infrastructure:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase (Firestore / Storage): Encrypted database and cloud storage located in EU regions with strict access control rules.
  • Brevo (formerly Sendinblue): Transactional SMTP infrastructure used exclusively by the Firebase Trigger Email Extension to deliver notification and status updates to our official mailbox.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data or creative ideas to any advertisers or third-party marketing networks.

5. Retention Period

Submission details are retained for up to 12 months to allow evaluation across upcoming festival production cycles, or until our collaboration agreement is formally finalized. If you request deletion, your records and attachments are permanently removed within 14 calendar days.

6. Your GDPR Rights

Under European data protection law (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request access to copies of your personal information.
  • Request rectification of any inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request erasure (the "Right to be Forgotten") of your submission and uploaded PDF files.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us directly at info@dominikphotofficial.lt with the subject line "GDPR Data Request".

7. Cookies & Analytics Note

This website does not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies or invasive user profiling scripts. Standard local storage (localStorage) is used solely to remember your preferred language (EN/LT) and prevent form spam (rate limiting).
