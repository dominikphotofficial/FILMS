Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Data Controller

Personal data submitted through films.dominikphotofficial.lt is controlled by Dominik Šuškevič (DP.FILMS), Lithuania. Contact: info@dominikphotofficial.lt.

2. Information Collected

  • Name, country/city, and preferred artistic role.
  • Chosen communication contact (Email, Telegram, Instagram, or Phone).
  • Film pitch synopses, portfolio links, and optional script files (.pdf, .doc, .docx, .txt).

3. Purpose & Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6)

Data is processed solely under your explicit affirmative consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) to review creative collaboration proposals, coordinate auditions or script reviews, and prepare bilateral film deal memos.

4. Data Storage & Email Notifications

Submissions and attachments are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) with strict access controls. Notification messages are queued in Firestore for email delivery to info@dominikphotofficial.lt. Data is never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

5. Retention & Your Rights

Submissions are retained for up to 12 months unless finalized into a production agreement or erased earlier upon request. To access, correct, or delete your submission, email info@dominikphotofficial.lt.
