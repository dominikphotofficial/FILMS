Profile

Filmmaker & Photographer

Dominik Šuškevič is the creator of the short film Frozen Time: What If (Director, Screenwriter, Producer, Cinematography), filmed at Cirkliškis Manor and awarded first place in the student category at the Ąžuolynas International Film Festival (30 April 2026, Kaunas International Gymnasium).

Since 2026, he operates under registered Individual Activity (Individuali veikla) for commercial filming projects alongside his independent short film collaborations and photography portfolio.
