FROZEN TIME: WHAT IF

Short Film · 2026 · Lithuania · Ąžuolynas International Student Film Festival

About the Film

"Frozen Time: What If" is an exploration of human stillness, winter environments, and contemplative time. The film investigates how a single pause in routine shifts personal perspective against cold, monumental natural geometry.

Shot entirely with ambient winter lighting, the film combines deliberate pacing, subtle sound layers, and wide-angle cinematography to create an introspective mood that won 1st Place recognition from the festival jury at AISFF 2026.
