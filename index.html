SPACE. LIGHT. EMOTION.

The visual triad guiding all my creative work: finding stillness in open space, working with natural ambient light, and letting authentic human emotions speak without artificial clutter.

I am a young filmmaker, cinematographer, and photographer. As a film director, I have directed exactly one film: the short production „FROZEN TIME: WHAT IF“ (2026), which was awarded 1st Place at the Ąžuolynas International Student Film Festival (AISFF).

I operate with deep respect for cinematography, organic color grading, and precise rhythm. In addition to narrative film work, I shoot atmospheric visual studies and nature cinema. You can also explore my dedicated photography portfolio at portfolio.dominikphotofficial.lt ↗.

Space

Deliberate composition, architectural awareness, and quiet room for characters to breathe.

Light

Natural daylight, gentle shadows, and rich chiaroscuro without heavy studio rigs.

Emotion

Raw honesty, visual tension, and genuine human presence that resonates long after the credits.

Simple Process

How It Works

No bureaucracy. Four simple steps to take an idea from your head to festival screens.

01

Fill in the Form

Tell me about yourself, your country, your chosen role, and share your pitch or portfolio link below.

02

Personal Reply

I review every single submission myself and reply by email or your chosen contact channel within 48 hours.

03

Group Chat & Call

We meet on a brief call, align our creative taste, brainstorm story angles, and assemble our crew.

04

We Make It Together

We develop the screenplay, produce the film remotely or on location, edit, polish, and submit to festivals.
