Biography

Dominik Šuškevič

Dominik Šuškevič

Filmmaker & Photographer

SPACE. LIGHT. EMOTION.

The visual triad guiding all creative work: finding stillness in open space, working with natural ambient light, and letting authentic human emotions speak.

Dominik Šuškevič is a filmmaker and photographer. As a film director, he has directed exactly one film: the short production „FROZEN TIME: WHAT IF“ (2026), which was awarded 1st Place at the Ąžuolynas International Student Film Festival (AISFF).

In addition to directing, Dominik produces atmospheric visual studies, nature videography, and still photography. His photography portfolio is accessible at portfolio.dominikphotofficial.lt.

Space

Compositional balance, architectural awareness, and deliberate spatial rhythm.

Light

Natural illumination, subtle gradation, and understated chiaroscuro.

Emotion

Raw presence and visual sincerity that lingers beyond the final frame.

Supporting and Empowering Independent Student Filmmaking

The Ąžuolynas International Film Festival (AISFF) is an inspiring platform dedicated to discovering, celebrating, and distributing emerging student and independent cinematic talent. Dominik Šuškevič's film „FROZEN TIME: WHAT IF“ won 1st Place at the festival in 2026.

Important Notice Regarding Festival Submissions

Film submissions for the Ąžuolynas International Film Festival are NOT accepted directly on this website. The purpose of this section is purely for festival promotion, audience distribution, and encouraging young creators and student filmmakers to take part.

To submit your film, register your entry, or review the official rules and open calls, please visit the festival's official pages directly:
