Biography & Profile

Dominik Šuškevič

Filmmaker, Cinematographer and Photographer based in Lithuania.

Directorial & Artistic Profile

I am a visual artist and cinematographer dedicated to the visual rhythm of Space, Light, and Emotion. My work focuses on organic composition, chiaroscuro lighting, and capturing human stillness without artificial distractions.

To date, I have directed exactly one film: the short visual piece "Frozen Time: What If", which earned 1st Place at the Ąžuolynas International Student Film Festival (AISFF 2026). Additionally, I have served as cinematographer and editor on over 10 narrative and landscape studies, as well as working professionally in photography and commercial video.

My current focus is building an international production team for my next short film project, collaborating with talented screenwriters, actors, directors, sound designers, and visual artists worldwide.
