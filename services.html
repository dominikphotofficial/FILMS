Collaboration Terms

How We Make a Film Together

Transparent roles, respect for original authorship, and guaranteed opening and closing screen credits across all festival and online screenings.

Open Roles Any Country · Remote or On-Set

Screenwriters

Short narrative scripts or treatments (5–15 pages) with visual clarity, silence, and strong human subtext.

Actors & Performers

Performers comfortable with understated, camera-first acting in Lithuanian, English, or international co-productions.

Directors & Co-Directors

Storytellers seeking a dedicated cinematographer, editor, and colorist to realize a shared narrative vision.

Sound Designers & Crew

Audio artists shaping ambient textures, sparse dialogue mixes, and restrained original scores.

What You Receive Mutual Guarantees
01 Guaranteed Screen Credit Opening & Closing Titles Every festival screening & release carries your official role title.
02 100% Author Copyright Full Moral & IP Ownership Writers and composers keep full copyright over original work.
03 Collaboration Deal Memo Signed Before Production Clear written scope, schedule, and credit terms agreed in advance.
04 Showreel Master Files High-Res Delivery Full-resolution film masters and frame stills provided for your portfolio.
