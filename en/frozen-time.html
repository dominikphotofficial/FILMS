01 Award

About the Festival & Film

At the Ąžuolynas International Film Festival (KTG) held on 30 April this year, my short film Frozen Time: What If won first place in the student category.

The festival opened with an opening welcome, followed by the film screenings. After all the films were screened, the winners were announced.

I would like to thank everyone who watched and supported this idea from the very beginning. The success of this film is not only recognition for me, but also proof that silence and sincere emotion can speak louder than any noise.

02 Concept

Essence of the Work

This work explores the moment when time simply stops — that cold winter silence in which you have to pause and look inward.

My main aim was to convey what happens inside when the outer world goes still and a person is left alone with their thoughts. I wanted to show the contrast between the coldness of the moment and the inner warmth released by memories.

The tree at the end of the video symbolizes the memories that bloom in our hearts.
