SPACE. LIGHT. EMOTION.

The creative triad: finding stillness in open space, working with pure ambient daylight, and letting authentic human emotions speak with honesty.

I am a young filmmaker and photographer with confirmed professional qualifications in videography, camera operation, and post-production. As a film director, I have directed exactly one film: the short narrative production „FROZEN TIME: WHAT IF“ (2026), which won 1st Place at the Ąžuolynas International Student Film Festival (AISFF).

I believe that great cinema is born from strong stories and genuine collaboration, not massive Hollywood budgets. Whether working remotely with screenwriters or filming on location, I bring precision, technical mastery, and creative commitment to every project.
