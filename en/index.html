About Me

I am Dominik Šuškevič — cinematographer, videographer, and filmmaker. My creative focus centers on visual storytelling where technical precision serves raw human emotion.

I direct short films, cinematic visual projects, and atmospheric nature studies. I prioritize natural light, organic motion, and disciplined pacing that invites viewers to inhabit the space. I continually explore new visual languages, refine my directing voice, and investigate the interplay of illumination and shadow.

“Cinema begins where coincidence ends — every ray of light and every second holds deliberate meaning.”

— Dominik Šuškevič
